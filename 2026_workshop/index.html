About the workshop

The SKKU–Rice Quantum Collaboration Workshop brings together researchers from Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU) and Rice University who work on quantum science and technology.

Topics include condensed-matter theory and open quantum systems, trapped-ion and superconducting-circuit experiments, RF and microwave technologies for quantum computing, AMO physics, and quantum materials.

The program consists of invited talks by faculty members from both universities and a poster session by graduate students. The workshop aims to share recent work and to identify opportunities for collaboration.

Organized by Sungkyunkwan University and Rice University.
